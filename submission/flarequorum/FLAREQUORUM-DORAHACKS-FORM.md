## Short product description

```text
FlareQuorum is a Coston2 confidential-procurement application. Buyers publish deterministic rules and escrow FTestXRP; approved vendors send encrypted price, delivery, warranty, and qualification terms to three registered FCC machines. The market accepts a winner only after two frozen machines sign the same result, then publishes a verifiable award and conserved settlement without exposing losing bids.
```
