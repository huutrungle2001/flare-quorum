## Reply 1 — Product usefulness

```text
Public bids expose vendor strategy. A private procurement server hides bids but also asks everyone to trust its winner.

FlareQuorum keeps losing price, delivery, warranty and qualification terms private while rules, commitments, result binding and settlement remain public.
```

## Reply 2 — Why FCC is essential

```text
The browser never supplies a winner.

All 3 tender-fixed FCC machines acknowledge each accepted encrypted bid. FCC checks eligibility and scores the public price/delivery/warranty rule; 2 matching registered-machine results are required to settle.
```
